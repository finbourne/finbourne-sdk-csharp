# Finbourne.Sdk.Lusid.Model.DepositInterestPaymentEvent

Event to signify the repayment of interest accrued against a deposit holding. Not applied when upserted  for a period the deposit settles partly or wholly in kind, as its scheduled events settle that period's interest.
> **Inherits from:** [InstrumentEvent](InstrumentEvent.md)

## Properties

| **PaymentDate** | **DateTimeOffset** | Optional | Date that the interest is due to be paid. |
| **ExDate** | **DateTimeOffset** | Optional | Date that the accrued interest is calculated up until. |
| **Currency** | **string** | Required | Currency of the repayment. |
