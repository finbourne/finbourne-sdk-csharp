# Finbourne.Sdk.Lusid.Model.RecallableCapitalExpiryEvent

The lapse of a recallable portion of an earlier distribution on a CapitalInterest: the amount leaves  RecallableCapital and joins NonRecallableCapital. Raised when the recall window of the source  distribution closes.
> **Inherits from:** [InstrumentEvent](InstrumentEvent.md)

## Properties

| **Date** | **DateTimeOffset** | Optional | The date the recall window closes. |
| **Amount** | **decimal** | Required | The recallable amount that lapses, in the commitment currency. Must be positive. |
